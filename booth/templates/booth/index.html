Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about your virtual booth.

A simple, phone-friendly experience for your customers, with your brand front and center.

01What is a Virtual Photo Booth?

It is a simple photo-taking link that opens right on your phone screen. Customers can take branded photos and photo strips anywhere, anytime, without any heavy photo machine.

02Do users need to download an app?

No. There is no app to download and no login needed. It opens instantly in regular web browsers like Chrome or Safari.

03How do customers open the booth?

They just scan the QR code placed on your shop counter, desk, or event screen using their normal phone camera.

04What physical equipment do we need?

None. You only need to print and place your QR code stand. Customers use their own smartphones to click and save photos.

05Can we customize the branding and details?

Yes. We customize the photo strip templates with your company logo, business name, phone number, address, and brand colors.

06How do customers get their final photos?

Right after taking the picture, the finished photo strip shows on their phone screen. They can save it straight to their gallery with one tap or share it on WhatsApp and Instagram.

07Where can this booth be used?

You can use it in retail shops, offices, customer lounges, studios, product launches, corporate events, and parties to promote your brand.

08How is customer privacy and photo storage handled?

Photos are processed directly on the customer’s phone. We do not keep or misuse personal photos on external servers unless you specifically request an admin gallery.

09How does the live booth work?
  1. You place your printed QR code on your counter or table.
  2. The customer scans the QR code with their mobile phone.
  3. The camera opens instantly on their screen with your branded frame.
  4. The customer takes photos, and the system instantly creates their personalized photo strip.
  5. The customer saves the photo to their phone and shares it with friends.
10What should a business do to get started?

Simply contact us through our website. Share your logo, company name, phone number, and requirements, and we will set up your custom booth and QR code right away.
